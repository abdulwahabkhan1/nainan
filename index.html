Chapter II

The atelier

Every Maison Orlov piece begins in our Geneva workshop, where master horologists assemble movements by hand under loupes — the same gestures perfected over four generations.

Our eyewear is shaped from seven-month-cured Italian acetate, polished against linen and finished with hand-set rivets. No piece leaves the bench until it has passed seventy-three inspections.

Chapter III

A century of seeing & keeping time

Mikhail Orlov believed time was the only true luxury — and that to measure it well was a form of devotion.

— From the founder's journals, 1924

What began in a single Geneva atelier in 1923 has become a quiet authority in horology and optical craft. Three generations later, every Orlov timepiece is still regulated by hand, every spectacle frame still cut, polished and fitted by named artisans.

We make few pieces. We make them slowly. And we make them to outlive us.
